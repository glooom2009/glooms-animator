# How to install Gloom's Animator on iPhone / iPad

The iOS version is not on the App Store, so you install it yourself with a free tool called **Sideloadly**. It takes about 5 minutes the first time.

**You need:**
- An iPhone or iPad
- A **Windows PC or Mac**
- A USB cable for your phone
- An Apple ID (a free one works)

> Don't have a computer? Use the [web version](#no-computer-use-the-web-version) instead.

---

## Step 1: Download the files (on your computer)

1. Download **`GloomsAnimator-iOS-1.1.1.ipa`** from the [Releases page](../../releases/latest).
2. Download and install **Sideloadly** from [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io).
3. **Windows only:** Sideloadly needs **iTunes** and **iCloud** installed from Apple's website (the non-Microsoft Store versions). Sideloadly tells you if they're missing.

## Step 2: Install it on your phone

1. Plug your iPhone into the computer and unlock it. If the phone asks **"Trust This Computer?"**, tap **Trust**.
2. Open **Sideloadly**. Your iPhone should show up at the top.
3. Drag **`GloomsAnimator-iOS-1.1.1.ipa`** into Sideloadly.
4. Type your **Apple ID email** in the box and click **Start**.
5. Enter your Apple ID password when it asks. It only goes to Apple, to sign the app for your phone.
6. Wait until it says **Done**.

## Step 3: Allow the app on your phone

1. **Turn on Developer Mode** (iOS 16 and newer):
   **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → On**, then let the phone restart and tap **Turn On**.
2. **Trust your Apple ID:**
   **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management** → tap your Apple ID email → **Trust**.
3. Open **Gloom's Animator** from your home screen.

---

## Good to know

- **With a free Apple ID, the app stops opening after 7 days.** That's Apple's rule. To fix it, plug in your phone and do Step 2 again. Your animations stay. In Sideloadly you can turn on **automatic refresh** so it renews by itself when your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.
- A free Apple ID can have **3 sideloaded apps** at a time.
- **Updating:** download the new `.ipa` and do Step 2 again. It installs over the old one.

## Problems?

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Phone doesn't show up in Sideloadly | Unlock the phone, tap **Trust**, try another cable/USB port. On Windows, reinstall iTunes from apple.com. |
| "Untrusted Developer" when opening | Do Step 3 → Trust your Apple ID. |
| App doesn't show any Developer Mode option | Install the app with Sideloadly first; the option appears after that. |
| App stopped opening | 7 days passed: do Step 2 again. |
| Error about two-factor / password | Use your normal Apple ID password and enter the code that pops up on your phone. |

---

## No computer? Use the web version

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone and go to:
   **https://glooom2009.github.io/glooms-animator/app/**
2. Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it from your home screen. It works like an app, with no 7-day limit.
